SELECTED SERIES

Through my lens

A few photo series shot between lines of code — streets, animal, strangers, and the lake.

01

Dachshund

The photo uses a close-up and shallow depth of field technique, keeping the visual focus entirely on the dog's face and clear eyes, while the background is softly blurred.

02

A traditional Japanese castle

In the center of the frame is a multi-tiered Japanese castle keep featuring white exterior walls and dark tiled roofs, situated atop a massive stone foundation (ishigaki) built from large rocks.

03

Ema-style wish tags

The photo uses a shallow depth of field. The plaques in the center are sharp, while the foreground at the left edge and the distant rows on the right are softly blurred. The diagonal composition of the rails leads the eye along the rows. The overall mood is lively and colorful, and it is also personal, since each tag is someone's private hope.

04

A calm lake on a bright, clear day

This is a tranquil lakeside boat rental dock. In the foreground, a white rowboat—its paintwork weathered and mottled, featuring a red heart painted on the side—is moored beside a gray wooden pier. The boat’s reflection shimmers in the blue-green water; the far shore is lined with lush pine and broad-leaved trees, set against a backdrop of blue sky and expansive white clouds. The overall atmosphere is fresh and serene, characterized by vivid colors.
